# Privacy Policy for Island Watch

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

Island Watch (孤岛守望, the "Game") is developed and operated by Dai Yun (戴赟), an individual developer based in Yancheng, Jiangsu, China ("I," "me," or "my"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you play the Game, interact with advertisements, or contact me.

This policy covers the Google Play edition of Island Watch, Android package **com.darrian.islandwatch**.

Island Watch is a single-player game with no account registration or login. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which involves network access and processing of certain device and usage information. Offline gameplay does not mean that the advertising services collect no data.

## 1. Information Handled by the Game

### Local gameplay data

The Game stores information such as your current level, permanent starting bonuses, diamond balance, permanent unlocks, capacity upgrades, reward-claim records, first-start state, and audio, graphics, and frame-rate preferences in its app-specific storage. These records support gameplay and restore the corresponding progress and settings on later launches. They are not a complete save of every unit or resource in an ongoing match.

The Game does not automatically send these local gameplay files to me and does not provide an account-based cloud save service.

### Local performance diagnostics

The Game may keep local performance records, including frame rate, memory use, and counts of game objects. When you run a performance diagnostic test, its local report may include your device model, operating system, rendering method, window dimensions, display refresh rate, CPU/GPU timing, and test results.

Selecting the option to copy diagnostic data writes the report to your clipboard; it does not read the clipboard's previous contents or automatically send the report to me. You decide whether to share it using email or another service. Local game diagnostics are separate from the information collected by AdMob described below.

### Information you voluntarily send

If you contact me, I receive your email address and any message, screenshot, or diagnostic report you provide. I use this information to respond, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy requests. Sending feedback is optional and is not required to play. Please review attachments and remove unrelated personal information before sending them.

## 2. Google AdMob

The Google Play edition uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. It does not integrate the TapADN advertising service used by a separate distribution build.

An advertisement is requested when you select an advertising reward action. Rewards are granted when the advertising SDK confirms that the reward conditions were met. You can play without choosing to watch these advertisements. Cancelling an ad or a failed ad request does not grant an advertising reward. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, available identifiers, and your choices, Google's advertising SDK collects and shares information including:

- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location.
- **Device and account identifiers**, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
- **App and advertising interactions**, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
- **Diagnostic information**, such as app and SDK performance, launch timing, and other technical measurements.

Google processes this information to deliver and measure advertising, perform advertising-related analytics, and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising, where enabled and permitted, may use information to select ads relevant to your interests.

Google explains its practices in the following resources:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## 3. Advertising Consent and Choices

At launch, UMP requests an update to your consent status and displays a consent form when required. The Game initializes the Mobile Ads SDK and requests a rewarded ad only after you select an advertising reward action and UMP indicates that ads may be requested. The consent-status check itself involves communication with Google, even before you choose to watch an ad. Permission to request an ad does not necessarily mean that you have consented to personalized advertising.

To review the policy or change available advertising choices, open **Settings > Privacy policy & ad settings**. When UMP requires a privacy options entry point, select **Ad privacy choices** to reopen the form and update or withdraw your choices. Updated choices affect subsequent advertising requests; they do not erase information already processed. Simply playing the Game does not replace consent where it is required.

Non-personalized advertising can still involve information processing for purposes such as frequency capping, reporting, and fraud prevention. It does not mean that all advertising-related data collection stops.

You can also manage available advertising ID controls in your Android device settings, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported. Options and menu names vary by Android version. These actions do not necessarily delete information already collected by Google.

If you need help using the privacy controls, contact **killerdayyun@gmail.com**. Google's own privacy controls and request mechanisms are described in its Privacy Policy.

## 4. How Information Is Used and Shared

Local gameplay information is used to run the Game and retain progress and preferences. Local diagnostic information helps you inspect performance and, if you choose to share a report, helps me investigate problems. Contact details and feedback are used to respond to you and handle your request.

Google receives advertising information through AdMob as described in Section 2. Local save files and locally generated diagnostic reports are not automatically uploaded to me as part of this process.

If you email me, the email services used by you and me process and store your message to deliver the correspondence. Their own privacy policies also apply.

I do not sell the feedback or contact details you send me or use them for advertising personalization. I may disclose information I hold where necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, or to protect users and address security incidents, only to the extent permitted by law.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

**Local data:** Gameplay progress, bonuses, diamonds, unlocks, reward-claim records, and settings remain in the Game's local storage until you clear its data or uninstall it. Diagnostic records may be replaced during later gameplay or testing. You can clear local data through your device's app settings, typically under **Settings > Apps > Island Watch > Storage > Clear data**. Menu names vary by device. This removes saved progress and settings; I cannot restore them from a developer-operated cloud account. Copies you export or share must be managed separately.

**Feedback and correspondence:** I retain messages and attachments for the time needed to investigate and resolve the issue and complete related correspondence. When that purpose is complete and there is no continuing legal or dispute-resolution need, I delete the information or remove information that identifies you. You may request earlier deletion by emailing me. If a legal obligation prevents deletion, I will explain the applicable limitation and restrict further use as required.

**Advertising data:** Google determines the retention of information it processes under its applicable policies. See [Google's retention information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). Clearing the Game's local data does not automatically erase information previously received by Google or messages already sent to me.

Because the Game has no user accounts, there is no Island Watch account to delete. You can still delete local data and request deletion of information you have sent me.

## 6. Security

Gameplay files and local reports are stored in the Game's app-specific directory. This does not mean that those files are separately encrypted. Information you send for support is used only as needed to address your inquiry and is not intentionally made public.

Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No transmission or storage method can eliminate every security risk. Please protect access to your device and avoid sharing sensitive information in diagnostic reports or support messages.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children. The meaning of a child and applicable age thresholds may vary by country. This statement describes the Game's intended audience; it does not mean that the Game verifies a player's age.

I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the Game or by contacting me, please email **killerdayyun@gmail.com**. I will investigate and take appropriate steps to delete information I hold that was collected without any legally required parental consent, and address relevant service-provider processing as appropriate.

The advertising consent form described in Section 3 is not a mechanism for verifying age or obtaining parental consent.

Google operates internationally and may process advertising information outside your country. Its Privacy Policy explains its international processing practices and applicable safeguards.
